Safety guide / 05

A few rules worth
remembering.

01

Password hygiene

Prefer long passphrases you can remember and unique passwords you do not reuse. A password manager can help create and store different credentials without asking you to memorize them all.

Check a sample ↗
02

Phishing prevention

Slow down when a message creates urgency. Check the full sender address, inspect the real destination of a link, and be suspicious of unexpected attachments or requests for OTPs.

Read the signals ↗
03

Privacy settings

Review who can see your profile, location, contacts, and posts. Apps should earn access to sensitive permissions — and you can change your mind later.

Set a baseline ↗
04

Safe browsing

Use official apps or typed addresses for important accounts. Keep your browser and operating system updated, and avoid signing in on devices you do not control.

If something feels wrong / 06

A calm response
beats a fast one.

If an account may be compromised, work through the steps in order. Use the official service website or app — not a link from a suspicious message.

  1. 01

    Change the affected password

    Use the service’s official app or typed web address, and choose a new password.

  2. 02

    Change reused passwords

    Any other account sharing the old password should get a unique one too.

  3. 03

    Review sessions and recovery settings

    Sign out unknown devices and check recovery email addresses and phone numbers.

  4. 04

    Enable multi-factor authentication

    Add a second layer using the account’s own security settings.

  5. 05

    Contact the official provider

    Use the provider’s verified support page if you see activity you do not recognize.
